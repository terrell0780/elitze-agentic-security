# ELITZE Brand and IP Policy

## Product brand

All product-facing identity belongs to **ELITZE Agentic Security**. Product pages, navigation, console surfaces, sales copy, screenshots, demos, and acquisition materials must use ELITZE naming and ELITZE visual identity.

Do not use another company's name, logo, badge, mascot, product identity, or community-project branding as the identity of an ELITZE feature.

## External references

External technologies, services, standards, APIs, or integrations may be referenced when technically or legally necessary. They must be described as dependencies, integrations, compatibility targets, or reference standards—not as ELITZE-owned products.

## Standards

Security framework names may be shown in compliance/reference contexts. They remain external standards. ELITZE owns the implementation, control workflow, evidence model, and product experience around any mapping.

## Third-party code and content

Do not copy third-party repositories, libraries, documentation, skill packs, images, logos, or other protected content into the proprietary ELITZE source unless the transfer rights and license have been reviewed and the required notices are preserved.

## Acquisition rule

A buyer receives only assets and rights that the seller actually owns and can legally transfer. No sale document may claim that third-party or licensed assets became exclusive merely because they appear in or connect to the product.
