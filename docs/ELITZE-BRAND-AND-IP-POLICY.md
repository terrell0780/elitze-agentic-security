# ELITZE Brand and IP Policy

## Product identity

Product-facing identity uses **ELITZE Agentic Security** and **ELITZE**. Product pages, navigation, console surfaces, documentation, screenshots, and demonstrations must use ELITZE naming and visual identity.

Do not use another company's name, logo, badge, mascot, product identity, or community-project branding as the identity of an ELITZE feature.

## External references

External technologies, services, standards, APIs, or integrations may be referenced when technically or legally necessary. Describe them as dependencies, integrations, compatibility targets, or reference standards—not as ELITZE-owned products.

## Standards

Security framework names may be shown in compliance/reference contexts. They remain external standards. ELITZE owns the implementation, control workflow, evidence model, and product experience around any mapping.

## Third-party code and content

Do not copy third-party repositories, libraries, documentation, skill packs, images, logos, or other protected content into the ELITZE source unless transfer rights and licenses have been reviewed and required notices are preserved.
