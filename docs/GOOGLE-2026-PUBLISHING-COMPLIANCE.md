# ELITZE — Google 2026 Publishing Compliance

This document is the ELITZE internal publishing boundary for public website copy, search content, structured data, and Google advertising. It does not claim Google approval or affiliation.

## Core rules

### 1. Truthful product and business representation

ELITZE must accurately describe the product, business identity, available services, qualifications, and offers. Do not imply Google affiliation, endorsement, partnership, special access, preferred placement, or program status that does not exist.

### 2. No unreliable or guaranteed outcomes

Do not claim guaranteed rankings, guaranteed traffic, guaranteed conversions, guaranteed security outcomes, or other improbable results. Product claims must match what the destination can actually demonstrate.

### 3. Offers and pricing must be real and available

An advertised offer must be available at the destination and the destination must make the relevant terms clear. Expired, unavailable, or mismatched offers must not be promoted.

### 4. Search content must provide real value

Public content should be helpful, reliable, original, relevant, and people-first. Do not create large volumes of low-value pages primarily to manipulate Search or generative Search visibility.

### 5. AI-assisted content

AI can assist research and structure, but published content must satisfy Google's Search Essentials and spam policies. ELITZE must not publish repetitive AI-generated pages with little original value.

### 6. Structured data

Structured data must accurately represent the visible page content and comply with Google's general and feature-specific guidelines. Structured data is not a guarantee of rich-result display.

### 7. Destination quality

Google Ads destinations must function on commonly used browsers/devices, be crawlable for ad review where required, be relevant to the advertisement, and provide useful destination content.

## ELITZE-specific prohibitions

Do not publish:

- fake customers;
- fabricated testimonials or reviews;
- invented ratings or rankings;
- invented customer counts;
- invented traffic or conversion metrics;
- invented uptime or performance statistics;
- fake security events, incidents, assets, agents, findings, reachability, or remediation results;
- mock or synthetic operational telemetry presented as real;
- claims that an unconfigured integration is live;
- claims that a planned control is implemented;
- claims that missing evidence proves safety;
- Google partnership, endorsement, ranking, placement, or access claims without a real authorized relationship.

## Product truth requirement

ELITZE production mode is **real-data-only**. The absence of connected evidence must result in an explicit unavailable state rather than a substitute data set.

## Verification rule

Before publishing any material that claims an ELITZE capability, price, offer, integration, customer outcome, ranking, or security result, verify the corresponding real source record or contractual fact. If it cannot be verified, the claim must not be published as established fact.
