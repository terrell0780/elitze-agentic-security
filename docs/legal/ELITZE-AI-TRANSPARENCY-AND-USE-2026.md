# ELITZE AI Transparency and Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 2026.09
**Effective date:** September 3, 2026

> Legal/compliance draft for counsel review. This policy describes product controls and contractual expectations; it is not a certification of regulatory compliance.

## 1. AI disclosure
Where ELITZE presents an interactive AI system to a user, the product should identify the interaction as AI-assisted where required by applicable law. Where ELITZE generates or materially alters content intended for publication, customers are responsible for applying legally required disclosures, labels, provenance metadata, or machine-readable markings.

## 2. Human oversight
Customers must maintain meaningful human oversight for decisions that can materially affect individuals, including employment, credit, insurance, housing, education, healthcare, legal status, safety, or access to essential services, unless a lawful and documented alternative applies.

ELITZE controls are intended to support governance and evidence; they do not replace required human decision-makers or legal review.

## 3. Agent authorization
Agents must operate with an identifiable principal, approved purpose, authorized tools, least-privilege credentials, target scope, and applicable approval policy. Customers should use short-lived credentials and explicit authorization for consequential actions.

## 4. Security operations
AI security testing, red-team activity, vulnerability validation, exploitability testing, and automated remediation must operate only against authorized targets and within approved scope.

## 5. Content integrity
ELITZE REO uses provenance and evidence gates. Missing provenance prevents automatic approval. Missing or unverifiable evidence requires review. Similarity and duplicate signals are treated as contextual integrity signals rather than proof of infringement or misconduct.

## 6. Model and provider governance
Customers should maintain an inventory of model providers, models, agents, tools, datasets, prompts, skills, and integrations used in production. Provider-specific data processing, retention, training, regional processing, safety, and acceptable-use terms must be reviewed before routing regulated or confidential data.

## 7. Training and customer data
The applicable enterprise agreement, privacy notice, and data-processing agreement determine whether Customer Content may be used for service improvement or model training. No product documentation should imply a training restriction that is not contractually and technically supported.

## 8. Regulatory change management
ELITZE will maintain this policy as AI laws, regulator guidance, model-provider terms, and technical standards evolve. Customers remain responsible for their own legal obligations and deployment-specific risk assessments.

## 9. 2026 regulatory context
As of September 3, 2026, the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations apply from August 2, 2026, and the European Commission has stated that enforcement of general-purpose AI provider obligations begins from that date. ELITZE does not represent that a customer's deployment is compliant merely because these controls are enabled.
