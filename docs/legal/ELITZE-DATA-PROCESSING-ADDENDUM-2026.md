# ELITZE Data Processing Addendum — Draft Schedule

Counsel-review draft. This document becomes binding only when incorporated into an executed agreement.

## 1. Roles and scope
The parties must identify, for each processing activity, whether the Customer is controller, processor or another legally applicable role and whether ELITZE is processor, subprocessor or another applicable role.

## 2. Processing details
The order form or schedule should identify subject matter, duration, purpose, categories of personal data, data subjects, systems, regions and documented Customer instructions.

## 3. Processing instructions
ELITZE processes personal data only to provide the contracted Services, maintain security, support the Services, comply with documented instructions and meet applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Confidentiality and security
Personnel and authorized subprocessors with access to personal data must be subject to confidentiality requirements. Technical and organizational measures are described in the Security Addendum and deployment schedule.

## 5. Subprocessors
Subprocessors require an appropriate contractual basis and security/privacy review. The live subprocessor register is the production source of truth. Changes follow the notice and objection mechanism in the negotiated agreement.

## 6. Rights assistance
Where legally applicable and technically reasonable, ELITZE supports the Customer's response to access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection requests.

## 7. Security incidents
ELITZE will notify the Customer of a confirmed security incident affecting Customer personal data according to the notification mechanism and timeframe in the negotiated agreement, subject to applicable law.

## 8. International transfers
The parties use the transfer mechanism required by applicable law and the negotiated agreement, including appropriate contractual safeguards where required.

## 9. Government requests
Where legally permitted, ELITZE will assess and handle government or law-enforcement requests using applicable legal process and contract terms.

## 10. Retention and deletion
At termination, ELITZE returns or deletes personal data according to the enterprise agreement, documented retention schedule and applicable legal obligations.

## 11. Audit and evidence
Customers may receive reasonable security and compliance evidence appropriate to the service tier and agreement. Independent reports remain subject to their actual scope and validity period.

## 12. Order of precedence
The executed enterprise agreement should specify precedence among the agreement, DPA, Security Addendum, order form and public policies.

## 13. Counsel completion items
Complete with actual legal entity names, addresses, contact details, governing law, dispute terms, transfer mechanisms, notice method and negotiated liability language.
