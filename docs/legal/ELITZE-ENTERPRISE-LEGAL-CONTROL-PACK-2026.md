# ELITZE Enterprise Legal & Security Control Pack — 2026

Counsel-review control framework. It does not itself create a binding contract or legal opinion. Signed agreements and applicable law control.

## Contract stack
1. Master / Enterprise Agreement or order form
2. Terms of Service
3. Data Processing Addendum
4. Security Addendum
5. Subprocessor Register
6. Acceptable Use Policy
7. Vulnerability Disclosure Policy
8. Incident Response and Security Incident Notification schedule
9. Service Level / Support terms where applicable
10. AI-specific terms and transparency notice
11. Customer responsibility / shared-responsibility matrix

## Canadian privacy baseline
The Canadian baseline distinguishes federal and provincial applicability. Controls include an accountable privacy owner, governance program, purpose/collection records, lawful consent or other authority where applicable, access/correction handling, retention/destruction, third-party processor safeguards, incident register, breach assessment and notification workflow, privacy impact/threat assessment where required, and evidence of safeguards appropriate to sensitivity.

For Quebec, separately track Law 25 governance, incident, assessment and transfer requirements.

## Global schedule
The applicability engine maps customers to GDPR, EU AI Act, NIS2, DORA, UK GDPR/Data Protection Act framework, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, India DPDP, Australia Privacy Act/APPs, Singapore PDPA, Japan APPI, South Korea PIPA and China PIPL.

## DPA requirements
Track controller/processor roles, processing details, instructions, confidentiality, technical/organizational measures, subprocessors, rights assistance, incident assistance, deletion/return, audit evidence, international transfer mechanism, government requests and precedence.

## Security Addendum
Document identity, MFA/SSO, tenant isolation, encryption, key management, vulnerability management, logging, incident response, recovery, secure development, SBOM/provenance, independent testing, infrastructure controls, subprocessors and data-residency evidence.

## Subprocessor controls
Production providers enter the live register only after contract, security and privacy review. Provider certifications identify issuer, scope and validity. Production changes create compliance-change events.

## Acceptable use
Prohibit unauthorized access/testing, credential theft, destructive activity, unlawful surveillance, security-control evasion, attacks outside approved scope and autonomous actions beyond granted authority.

## Vulnerability disclosure
Maintain a real security contact, intake, triage, severity, remediation, retest and coordinated-disclosure process. Safe-harbor language is limited to good-faith activity within scope.

## Incident response
Maintain detect -> triage -> contain -> preserve -> investigate -> remediate -> validate -> notify -> close -> learn, with jurisdiction-specific privacy/legal assessment.

## AI and agentic use
Address model routing, version changes, customer authorization, agent identity, tool/MCP/A2A reach, autonomous actions, approvals, prompt injection, evidence, customer validation of consequential outputs, disclosure/labeling and third-party provider terms.

## Adversarial testing
Pentera-class and other active validation require written authorization, targets, exclusions, limits, stop conditions, emergency stop, evidence and retest.

## Claim firewall
Prohibit policy document -> legal compliance claim; code -> certification; automated test -> independent penetration test; vendor certificate -> customer-tenant compliance; connector -> live production integration; simulated attack -> real attack evidence; framework mapping -> certification; stale evidence -> current claim.

No evidence, no claim. No current evidence, no current claim.

## Proposed Canadian contract default
If counsel approves a Canada-first standard form, a proposed default can specify British Columbia law and applicable federal laws of Canada, with dispute venue in British Columbia, subject to mandatory law and the negotiated enterprise agreement. This is a counsel decision, not a final contractual term.

## Final release gate
Before a claim is marked verified: scope known, applicability determined, control implemented, evidence attributable and current, reviewer identified, external assurance separately identified, residual risk separately recorded, and legal/contract owner approval obtained where required.
