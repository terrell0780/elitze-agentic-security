## Purpose

It is **not** a legal opinion and does not itself transfer title, establish copyright registration, or prove ownership.

## Control evidence

### Repository control

The canonical GitHub repository is maintained under the ELITZE project account identified by the repository path above. Repository access/control is evidence of repository administration, but repository control alone is not conclusive proof of legal intellectual-property title.

Recommended preserved evidence:
- repository URL and repository owner/account
- commit history and signed commits where available
- branch protection and access-control configuration
- release tags
- CI run history
- issue/PR history
- contributor list
- repository export or archive at transaction diligence time

### Domain control

Preserve current registrar/DNS evidence showing control of:
- elitze.info
- www.elitze.info

Domain control is separate from trademark ownership and software copyright ownership.

### Software authorship and contribution provenance

For each contributor, commissioned developer, contractor, employee, or collaborator, preserve the applicable agreement or documented legal basis covering:
- copyright ownership or assignment
- work-made-for-hire treatment where legally applicable
- confidentiality
- invention/IP assignment where applicable
- open-source compliance
- permission to use contributed code/assets
- termination/survival terms

No contributor should be represented as having assigned rights unless the signed agreement or other authoritative evidence supports the statement.

### AI-assisted development

Where AI tools were used in development, retain:
- tool/provider identity
- relevant account/workspace records
- dates and project context
- human review/edit history
- source provenance
- any applicable provider terms
- confirmation that third-party confidential material was not improperly incorporated

AI assistance does not, by itself, establish or transfer ownership of the resulting software.

### Third-party software and content

Maintain a software bill of materials and license review for all dependencies and bundled assets.

Required rights states:
- OWNED
- LICENSED
- AUTHORIZED
- REVIEW_REQUIRED
- UNKNOWN

Only OWNED, LICENSED, or AUTHORIZED items should be treated as cleared for the corresponding use. AGPL/GPL, commercial, proprietary, trademarked, dataset, model, font, image, and API terms require their own license/terms review.

Third-party provider names and marks remain third-party property unless ELITZE has documented rights.

## Claim firewall

The application must never convert a missing document into a legal ownership PASS.

Use these claim states:
- **VERIFIED** — authoritative evidence reviewed and current for the stated scope.
- **EVIDENCE_PENDING** — evidence requested but not yet verified.
- **REVIEW_REQUIRED** — legal or licensing review is needed.
- **UNKNOWN** — ownership/rights basis is not established.
- **EXPIRED** — formerly valid evidence is no longer current.
- **BLOCKED** — publication or transfer should not proceed.

## Transaction boundary

A buyer transfer is only complete when the applicable definitive transaction documents are executed and the agreed accounts/assets are transferred through the appropriate legal and operational process.

Until then, this register is a diligence aid rather than evidence of completed ownership transfer.

## Pre-sale IP clearance actions

Before marketing ELITZE as an owned and transferable asset, preserve an evidence chain for every material component:

1. Canonical source snapshot and Git history.
2. Contributor, contractor and employment agreements or other valid assignment/ownership instruments.
3. Open-source dependency and license inventory.
4. Third-party API, model, dataset, image, font and content terms.
5. Domain registrar and DNS control evidence.
6. Brand/trademark clearance search in the jurisdictions where ELITZE will be used.
7. Any filed or registered trademark/copyright records, if applicable.
8. Definitive sale/assignment documents prepared for buyer diligence.

Canadian intellectual-property guidance distinguishes authorship from ownership and recognizes transfer/assignment as part of the ownership chain. Trademark registration is separate from domain or company-name registration. These are diligence principles, not a legal opinion.

## Public claim rule

Use the following language unless authoritative evidence supports stronger wording:

- ELITZE brand — product identity used by the software.
- ELITZE-owned — only where the rights record supports ownership.
- ELITZE-licensed — where a valid license supports the use.
- third-party — where the underlying rights remain external.
- review required — where the legal basis is incomplete.

Do not publish registered trademark, exclusive owner, copyright owner, patent, certified, or similar legal-status claims merely because code, domains, repositories, or branding exist.
