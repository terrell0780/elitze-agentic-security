# ELITZE Privacy Notice

**Version:** 2026.09
**Effective date:** September 3, 2026

> Privacy/legal draft for counsel review. The actual data practices of each deployment, integration, and customer contract control where they differ from this general notice.

## 1. Information covered
ELITZE may process account and contact information, authentication and authorization metadata, service configuration, security telemetry, API and integration metadata, support communications, usage information, Customer Content, and audit/evidence records required by the configured Services.

## 2. Purposes
Information may be processed to provide and secure the Services, authenticate users and agents, enforce policies, investigate security events, maintain audit records, provide support, prevent abuse, measure service operation, and satisfy legal obligations.

## 3. AI processing
Where AI models or automated systems are used, inputs and outputs may be processed to provide the requested function. Model-provider routing and processing depends on the configuration and applicable enterprise agreement, privacy terms, and provider terms.

ELITZE will not represent that data is excluded from model training unless that restriction is supported by the applicable contract and service configuration.

## 4. Retention and deletion
Retention varies by data category, customer configuration, service function, security requirements, and legal obligations. Enterprise customers may have contractual retention and deletion terms that control.

## 5. Subprocessors and integrations
ELITZE may use infrastructure, hosting, analytics, communications, security, AI model, and other subprocessors or third-party services necessary to operate the Services. Applicable enterprise agreements and data-processing terms govern authorized subprocessors.

## 6. International processing
Data may be processed in jurisdictions where ELITZE, its affiliates, subprocessors, or customer-selected providers operate, subject to applicable contractual safeguards and law.

## 7. Customer responsibilities
Customers are responsible for configuring integrations, access controls, data-routing policies, retention settings, and authorized processing. Customers must provide required notices and obtain required permissions for personal data submitted to the Services.

## 8. Security
ELITZE uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. Security controls cannot eliminate all risk.

## 9. Individual rights
Where applicable law grants rights concerning personal information, requests may be made through the privacy or contractual contact channel supplied to the requester. Verification and applicable legal exceptions may apply.

## 10. Changes
This Notice may be updated to reflect changes in law, regulation, technology, Services, or data practices. The effective date will identify the current version.
