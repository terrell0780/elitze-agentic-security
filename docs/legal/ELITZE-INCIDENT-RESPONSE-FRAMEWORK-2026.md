# ELITZE Security Incident Response & Notification Framework — Draft

Operational framework. Contractual and statutory notice duties control where they differ.

## Lifecycle
detect -> triage -> contain -> preserve -> investigate -> remediate -> validate -> notify -> close -> learn

## Required record
Incident identifier, detection timestamp, affected tenant/service, severity, owner, status, systems/data affected, timeline, indicators/evidence, containment actions, recovery actions, legal/privacy assessment, customer notification decision, regulator notification decision, communication owner, remediation actions, retest evidence and post-incident review.

## Privacy incident branch
1. Identify information and affected individuals/classes.
2. Assess likelihood and severity of harm under applicable law.
3. Preserve evidence.
4. Record the assessment and decision.
5. Notify required parties/authorities when the legal threshold and deadline are met.
6. Track customer and regulator communications.
7. Retain the record according to applicable requirements.

## Canadian controls
For PIPEDA-covered processing, maintain a breach-assessment record and decision trail. For Quebec processing within the applicable private-sector regime, maintain the incident register and required notification assessment.

## Global controls
The same incident object supports jurisdiction-specific assessments for GDPR/UK GDPR, NIS2, DORA, CCPA/CPRA and other applicable regimes without assuming one universal deadline.

## Evidence
Incident status is never derived from a UI badge alone. The underlying timeline, evidence and notification decision must be recorded and attributable.
