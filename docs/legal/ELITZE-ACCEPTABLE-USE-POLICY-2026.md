# ELITZE Acceptable Use Policy — Draft

Counsel-review draft. The executed enterprise agreement controls where it differs.

Customers and authorized users may use ELITZE only for lawful, authorized security, governance, compliance, software, operational and business purposes.

## Prohibited uses
- unauthorized access or testing;
- attacks outside an approved scope;
- credential theft or secret exfiltration;
- malware deployment or destructive activity;
- evasion of access controls or monitoring without authorization;
- unauthorized surveillance;
- disruption of availability or integrity;
- unlawful or abusive activities;
- autonomous actions beyond granted authority;
- active validation against targets outside authorized scope.

## Active security validation
Before active testing, the operator must have authorization, defined targets, exclusions, appropriate credentials, impact/rate limits, stop conditions, emergency-stop capability and evidence/audit requirements.

ELITZE may block, suspend or require review for activity that materially violates this policy or creates material security, legal or safety risk, subject to the governing agreement and law.

## Customer responsibility
The Customer remains responsible for authorization, lawful basis, data rights, target permissions, integration credentials and use of outputs.
