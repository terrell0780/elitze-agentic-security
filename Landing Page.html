Security work loses time when tools do not connect.

Teams often move between visibility, scanning, response, and review tools while pressure increases. ELITZE is built around one practical question: what can we see, contain, and safely change right now?

Keep context attached

Threat records retain attacker origin, target asset, mode of entry, and defense action rather than forcing responders to reconstruct each step.

Make controls reviewable

Whitelist-oriented boundaries and human approval gates support automated and agent-assisted actions without removing oversight.

Preserve custody

Customer-managed KMS, zero-egress options, and append-only Merkle audit chains help teams preserve technical control of sensitive environments.
