          FAQ

          Is anything on this page simulated?

          No. The health value, the KEV catalog version, the 14 findings, the CVSS/EPSS numbers, the SPF and DMARC records and the scan id all come from real calls made while building the page. Where a value could not be measured it reads UNMEASURABLE or UNKNOWN.

          Why is the platform health 503?

          Because the deployed app cannot reach its database. The honest state is "unavailable" with productionDatabase: NOT_VERIFIED; when the connection is verified the tile changes and so does this answer.

          Does the scanner touch my production systems?

          No. It reads manifests and lockfiles in a directory you name, then queries public advisory feeds. Reachability into running systems requires authorized validation, separately scoped.

          Do you auto-apply patches?

          No. The engine produces a plan — target version, command, verification, rollback, blast radius. Execution stays with a human or an approved CI job.

          Why does one finding have no fix?

          Because every advisory fix for that package is older than the installed version. Recommending it would be a downgrade, so the plan is withheld and the finding says so.

          Is Sara the same as Lindy?

          No. Sara is Elitze's security assistant. The agent surface borrows the shape people expect from workflow agents — triggers, email triage, scheduling, approvals — but it operates inside tenant authorization and cannot self-grant.

          Do you hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

          No. None held, stated plainly on the trust page.

          How do I pay?

          Interac e-Transfer or KOHO deposit. Google Pay requires a merchant processor with identity verification and is being enabled.
